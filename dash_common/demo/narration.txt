# Narration for the demo video. One line per caption:
#
#   start_frame : still_frame : still_name : text
#
# The caption runs from its start frame until the next entry's start frame, so
# these must stay in order. The still frame is the one copied into stills/ as a
# representative shot of that phase. Frame numbers are the same ones demo.lisp
# uses for its phases, so changing a phase there means changing it here.
0:20:pulling-away:Pedalling away from a standstill. Assist follows crank torque and cadence.
30:50:steady-assist:Steady. The motor is adding about twice the rider's own power.
60:72:speed-taper:Into the speed taper. The rider keeps working; assist is pulled back.
80:88:braking:Brake applied. Assist is cut at once, bypassing the ramp.
95:102:walk-assist:Walk assist. Walking pace with the cranks stopped, held by a button.
110:118:regen:Coasting downhill. The pack is taking current back.
125:130:fan:The cooling fan is running, read back from the auxiliary output.
135:138:kill-switch:Kill switch active. It outranks every other indicator.
142:152:chart:A rolling ten second chart. Any live value, autoscaled to the window.
160:172:settings:Controller settings, changed from the dash. Starred rows need the kill switch.
178:186:trip:Trip page: range, distance, energy and efficiency.
196:204:session:Session page: the maxima since the last reset.
214:222:battery:Battery page: a 20 series pack, cell voltages and the charge counters.
232:240:live:Live page: any four values, each chosen in settings.
246:256:cells:One bar per cell, scaled to the spread rather than to an absolute range. One cell is 0.3 V down; the outlined column is it.
266:278:shade:The quick shade, a swipe down from any page. Six buttons, each one an action, showing what its control is doing.
286:294:signals:Hazard and high beam, asked for over CAN. With nothing on the bus reporting back, the strip shows what was requested.
296:305:hold-chart:Holding a live cell fills its highlight...
310:313:chart-picked:...and opens the chart already plotting that cell.
316:322:theme-night:Night: every colour pulled down and towards red, so a bright panel does not cost dark adaptation.
328:334:theme-light:Light: the only pale background, with the status colours darkened to stay legible on it.
340:350:colour-rules:A colour rule per cell. Ramp, heat, low-is-bad for a state of charge, and green for a negative value.
